📖 Instructions ▾

1. Quick heads up on how this works

📱 This isn't an app store app, it's a website saved to your phone

🔑 Opening it on a new phone, or your saved login's gone? No problem — just enter your name and PIN, and you're straight back into your real account, same points, same history, nothing duplicated. Use the exact name you first signed up with, not a nickname or short version, or it won't find you

👉 Still worth adding it to your Home Screen (Share icon on iPhone, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, ⋮ menu, then "Install app" or "Install and create shortcut" — if it then asks you to choose, pick "Install", not "Create shortcut", that second one just opens in Chrome and won't actually work) — that way it stays logged in day to day, and it's what lets notifications work

🔄 Something looking off? Close the app fully and reopen it, that usually sorts it

🔔 Once you've added it to your Home Screen, head to Profile and turn on notifications, you'll get a nudge if you're about to miss a prediction, and a heads up once results are in

🚫 Says notifications are blocked, even though your phone settings say they're allowed? On Android, open Chrome itself (not the app), go to the site, tap the small icon next to the address bar, then Permissions, and switch Notifications to Allow there

2. Making a prediction

🎯 Enter a score for each fixture before kick-off, then tap Save Prediction

📅 All 46 games are already loaded — use the ‹ › arrows to flick between months, do them all in one go or dip in and out

🔒 Locks the second the match kicks off, no editing after that — get it in early if you won't be free nearer the time

👀 Once it locks, it moves out of here — check Picks for the result and how everyone called it

3. First Pompey goalscorer

⚽ Only Portsmouth players count — who scores for Pompey, not the opposition

🤷 Not sure? Leave it as "No Pompey goalscorer"

4. How points work

+3 exact score   +1 correct result   +2 first Pompey goalscorer

📊 Max 5 points a match — exact replaces correct, they don't stack

🏆 Level on points? Most exact scores wins, then most correct goalscorers

🏆 Cup games (Carabao Cup, FA Cup) — 90 minutes only, extra time and penalties don't count

💰 Prizes only go to verified entrants, whatever your league position

5. Prize structure

💷 £10 to enter, 10% covers admin, the rest is the prize pool — more winners and bigger prizes as more people join, see the League for the live numbers

✅ Only paid, verified entrants get paid out — make sure you've paid via the PayPal Pool

🅿️ No PayPal account? Tap "Contribute as a Guest" on the PayPal page, no sign up needed

6. Your mobile number

🔒 Only Ant James can see it, no one else

📞 Just so he can reach you if there's a query — any issues, message him direct

7. Around the app

📊 Your Season So Far — top of your Profile, shows your current rank, points, exact scores, correct results and correct goalscorers at a glance

🏆 League — live prize pool up top, full table below, tap any name for their full history

🏅 Season Records — sits under the League, tracks the best streaks and scores of the season

👀 Picks — its own tab, shows what everyone called once a match kicks off, grouped by scoreline

📊 Prediction Split — inside Picks, shows how everyone's split on the next fixture

🥇 League Awards — also inside Picks: Most Exact Scores, Most Correct Results (includes exact scores, since those are correct too), Most Correct First Goalscorers, Best Called Match, Most Predicted Scorer, and the League Average points per game

📢 Updates — up top, flags anything new, and it's where you can suggest a feature too

🔗 Share — also up top, get a QR code and link to invite someone

🔑 Forgotten your PIN? Just message Ant, he can sort you a new one

📲 Regularly switch between two devices? Sign out of one before logging into the other, otherwise whichever you used most recently becomes the active one and predictions made on the other stop counting

⚠️ Symbol next to a name? Just a possible duplicate signup Ant's sorting — nothing to worry about

8. If it all goes wrong

💸 System failure mid-season? The league's cancelled and everyone gets their money back via the PayPal Pool, as fast as possible
