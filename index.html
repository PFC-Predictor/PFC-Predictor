Using This App

Get it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): tap the three lines next to the address bar, tap Share, scroll down the Share sheet, tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add.

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app", and confirm.

What's in the app

Fixtures: upcoming matches, meet and kick-off times, and the venue.

Results: final scores, goal scorers, Player of the Match, and a match video link when one's been shared.

Player Stats: appearances, goals, clean sheets and POTM awards for the whole squad.

Squad: photos and squad numbers — tap a player to see their own stats.

Scores update automatically while a match is live, no need to refresh.
